\chapter{Introduction and Literature Review}

State the purpose of the research, review the literature that gives context to your
research questions \citep{smith2020}, and explain the rationale for your methods,
including your hypotheses or objectives.

\section{Background}

Spell out each term the first time you use it, unless it is a standard unit or
appears in your list of abbreviations. Use SI units, with a space between the number
and the unit, for example 25~\textdegree C or 4.5~mL.

Cite each kind of source the same way; references.bib shows how to enter each type.
Use \verb|\citep| for a citation in parentheses and \verb|\citet| when the authors
are part of the sentence. For example, \citet{jones2019} describes the general
principles (a book); low-cost temperature loggers were tested in small streams
\citep{arsenault2022} (a conference paper); a regional monitoring guide
\citep{ecoatlantic2025} (a web page, with the date it was accessed); and earlier
records are available as a published dataset \citep{lee2024data} (a miscellaneous
source). Several sources can go in one citation
\citep[e.g.][]{smith2020, arsenault2022}.
